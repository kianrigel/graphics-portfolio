THE INTRODUCTION / 001

Think like a developer.
Create like an artist.

I'm Kian Rigel Perez, a third-year Computer Science student at City College of Calamba. My practice lives at the intersection of graphic design, front-end engineering, and visual identity.

I approach every brief as a system: understand the problem, find the story, then build something both meaningful and memorable. From brand marks and editorial layouts to interactive digital interfaces, each output is an opportunity to make design work harder.
